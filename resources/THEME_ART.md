# Theme artwork

The eight images in `src/renderer/assets/theme-art/` were created for Sabri with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The app bundles optimized WebP copies and loads them locally.

Each request used this prompt framing.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original landscape illustration used as a decorative panel for a study planner theme. [Scene below.] Editorial-quality spatial composition, rich but restrained, clearly recognizable in a narrow 420 × 200 crop. Put the primary subject in the center-right and use foreground and background depth. Full bleed, 3:2 or wider landscape. No text, no interface, no borders, no labels, no watermarks, no trademarks.

- `light.webp` — A lilac notebook, fountain pen, and translucent lilac paperweight arranged in an elegant cream studio, soft window light, off-white lavender accents.
- `dark.webp` — An elegant dark navy university study nook at midnight, open notebook and desk lamp, indigo shadows, quiet rain-lit blue window.
- `f1.webp` — An original unbranded red open-wheel racing car parked at a track corner with a black and white curb, carbon-fiber textures and a late-evening pit light. No known team colors beyond red and charcoal, no sponsor marks.
- `cyberpunk.webp` — A rain-soaked elevated futuristic train platform, neon magenta and cyan architecture, city reflected in pavement, no signage, off-black shadows.
- `nature.webp` — A miniature tranquil cedar forest with a stone path, moss and soft shafts of morning light, layered deep emerald trees.
- `football.webp` — A modern unbranded football stadium under emerald-tinted floodlights, neatly cut green pitch and geometric terraces, no players, logos or signs.
- `anime.webp` — An original manga-inspired rooftop study desk with a notebook and pink cherry blossom petals against a muted lavender skyline, graphic cel-style shading; no characters.
- `islamic.webp` — Intricate Islamic geometric lattice casting warm gold light across a quiet architectural archway, subtle emerald ceramic tiles, respectful abstract arabesque geometry; no text, no writing.
